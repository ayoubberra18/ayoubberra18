Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Ayoub Auto Shop AI provides AI-assisted receptionist and appointment communication services for auto repair shops. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when customers interact with the service by phone or SMS.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that a customer provides during a phone call or text conversation, including name, phone number, vehicle year, make and model, requested service, requested appointment date and time, and other information needed to handle the customer's request.

How We Use Information

Information is used to answer customer questions, process appointment requests, send confirmations or scheduling updates, communicate service-related information, and help the relevant auto repair shop respond to the customer.

SMS and Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and consent records are used only to provide requested transactional communications such as appointment requests, confirmations, rescheduling, cancellations, and service updates.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS consent data, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or transferred for third-party marketing.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Customers may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

Service Providers

We may use service providers that help operate the receptionist, telephony, messaging, workflow automation, and data systems. These providers process information only as needed to deliver the service and are not authorized to use SMS opt-in data for their own marketing.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain appointment records, resolve support issues, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access or misuse.

Your Choices

You may decline SMS communications when speaking with the receptionist. If you previously opted in, you may reply STOP at any time to stop SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Children

This service is intended for customers seeking auto repair services and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy or SMS-related questions, contact the business using the phone number associated with the AI receptionist service.
